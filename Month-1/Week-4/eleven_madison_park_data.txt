Title: About — Eleven Madison Park
Content:
Welcome to Eleven Madison Park

Eleven Madison Park is a fine dining restaurant in the heart of New York City. Overlooking Madison Square Park, the restaurant is located at the base of a historic Art Deco building on the corner of East 24th Street and Madison Avenue.

Since opening in 1998, the restaurant has evolved into one of the world's most acclaimed dining destinations. Chef Daniel Humm has owned the restaurant since 2011.

In 2021, Eleven Madison Park transitioned to a fully plant-based menu using no animal products. The restaurant partners with Magic Farms, which grows produce exclusively for its seasonal menus.

Guests can enjoy:
- Full tasting menu
- Five-course menu
- Bar tasting menu
- À la carte snacks
- Wine pairings
- Cocktails

Title: Team — Eleven Madison Park
Content:
Daniel Humm is a Swiss chef, author, speaker, and owner of Daniel Humm Hospitality. He owns Eleven Madison Park, Clemente Bar, and Eleven Madison Home.

He earned his first Michelin star at age 24. Eleven Madison Park has received four stars from The New York Times, seven James Beard Foundation Awards, and three Michelin stars for more than twelve consecutive years.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, Daniel Humm partnered with Rethink Food and transformed the restaurant into a commissary kitchen that prepared more than one million meals.

In 2021 he reopened Eleven Madison Park with a fully plant-based menu.
